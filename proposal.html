Appointment Prep Notebook

Problem Domain

Short medical appointments are often brief and high-pressure. Patients get only a few minutes to talk through complex health concerns. Many cannot easily bring up clear symptom timelines, steady severity descriptions, and planned questions for their provider. English language learners feel this burden most strongly. They must work harder to follow medical terms and describe how they feel. Small but important details can be forgotten, and this adds stress for anyone living with ongoing or chronic health issues.

Existing digital tools fail to fill this specific gap. MyChart patient portals are built primarily for viewing lab test results and sending simple messages to clinic staff, while WebMD Symptom Checker focuses only on preliminary condition suggestions. Neither tool offers dedicated, structured note space to organize symptoms and prepare targeted questions ahead of an upcoming appointment. This creates a meaningful equity gap: users with language barriers, memory challenges, or long-term illness are disproportionately likely to leave appointments without communicating all their relevant concerns to their care provider, which may lead to incomplete or less effective care plans.

App Description

Our app, Appointment Prep Notebook, is designed to help people prepare for their doctor appointments. Our main users are patients who have trouble remembering their symptoms or questions during a short appointment. This can be especially helpful for people who do not speak English as their first language because explaining health problems in another language can be difficult. We want to create a simple website that makes it easier for patients to organize their information before seeing a doctor.

Interactive Features

The app will have two main interactive features and one smaller feature:

  • Symptom log: Users can add their symptoms, including the date, severity, and tags. They can also edit or delete their previous entries to keep their information updated.
  • Appointment Questions: Users can create a list of questions for an upcoming doctor appointment. They can add new questions, edit existing ones, and remove questions they no longer need.
  • Visit Summary (Small Feature): Users can select symptoms and questions from their saved entries and generate a printable summary to bring to their appointment.

The information in our app will mainly come from users themselves, so we do not need an external dataset. The app will save their symptom records and question lists so they can access them again later. These features work together because users can record their symptoms, prepare questions based on those symptoms, and organize everything into one summary. Our goal is not to diagnose health problems, but to help patients communicate with their doctors more clearly and make better use of their appointment time.
